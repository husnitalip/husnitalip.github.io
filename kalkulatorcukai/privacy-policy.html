Kalkulator Cukai – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · App: Kalkulator Cukai (com.hsklm.tax_calculator_my) · Developer: Husni Talip

Summary

Kalkulator Cukai keeps all of your tax information on your phone. There is no account, no sign-up and no developer server. The developer never receives your income, receipts, receipt photos, name or tax number.

Information stored on your device

The income, PCB/MTD, marital status, number of children, relief receipts, receipt photos, e-Invoice numbers, name and tax reference number you enter are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. This information leaves your device only when you choose to share a PDF report or CSV file yourself, for example by email or a messaging app. You can delete it at any time with Padam semua data in the app, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the app.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo picker are used only when you choose to attach a photo of a receipt. The selected photo is copied into the app's private folder and is never uploaded.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), app interactions and diagnostic information. Google processes this information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Where required, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads. Pro subscribers do not see ads.

Subscriptions

Kalkulator Cukai Pro is a monthly subscription purchased through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment. The developer does not receive your card or payment details. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No tax data, receipts or photos are sent to the developer or any server operated by the developer.
  • No access to contacts or precise location.
  • No personal data is sold.

Children

Kalkulator Cukai is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Not tax advice

Kalkulator Cukai provides estimates only. It is not affiliated with the Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia (LHDN / HASiL). Refer to hasil.gov.my for official information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Dasar Privasi (Bahasa Melayu)

Kalkulator Cukai menyimpan semua maklumat cukai anda dalam telefon anda sahaja. Tiada akaun dan tiada pelayan pembangun. Pendapatan, PCB, status, bilangan anak, resit, gambar resit, nombor e-Invois, nama dan nombor cukai yang anda masukkan disimpan dalam storan peribadi aplikasi. Maklumat ini hanya keluar dari peranti apabila anda sendiri berkongsi laporan PDF atau fail CSV. Anda boleh memadamnya melalui Padam semua data atau dengan menyahpasang aplikasi.

Kamera dan galeri digunakan hanya untuk gambar resit yang anda pilih, dan gambar tidak dimuat naik. Versi percuma memaparkan iklan Google AdMob. Google mungkin mengumpul ID pengiklanan, alamat IP (lokasi anggaran), interaksi aplikasi dan data diagnostik untuk iklan. Pelanggan Pro tidak melihat iklan. Bayaran langganan Pro diproses sepenuhnya oleh Google Play.

Aplikasi ini untuk pengguna dewasa (18+), memberikan anggaran sahaja, dan tidak berkaitan dengan LHDN.
