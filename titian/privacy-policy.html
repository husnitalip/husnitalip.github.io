Titian – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Titian ("the app") shows live road traffic between Kulim, Kulim Hi-Tech Park and the Penang Bridge, Malaysia, in both directions. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Summary

There is no account and no sign-in. The app does not collect, store or share any personal information. It does not read your location, contacts, photos or files, and it shows no ads.

Traffic data

Our server (hosted by Supabase) fetches current speeds from TomTom for fixed points on the roads the app covers, on a schedule. These points are built into the app and are the same for every user; nothing about you or your phone is sent to TomTom.

When you open the app, it downloads the latest traffic readings from our server. Like any website, the server receives your device's IP address as part of the connection. The app does not send any other information, and we do not use the IP address to identify you.

Location

The app does not ask for or read your phone's location. The map shows the same roads to everyone.

Links

The "Official highway cameras" button opens the Lembaga Lebuhraya Malaysia website in your browser. That site has its own privacy policy.

Data shared

We do not collect or sell personal data. Supabase hosts the traffic readings to run the app.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children

The app is a general traffic app for drivers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
