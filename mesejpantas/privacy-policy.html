MesejPantas — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Summary

MesejPantas lets you save message templates and send them through WhatsApp. Your templates and what you type stay on your phone. We do not run any server and we do not collect your personal data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

Your templates, the values you last typed into them (for example a name, a reason or a phone number) and your settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

2. Sending messages

When you tap Send, MesejPantas opens WhatsApp (or your share menu) with the message filled in. The message is then handled by WhatsApp or the app you choose, under that app's own privacy policy. MesejPantas does not read your chats or contacts.

3. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google: your device's IP address (which can indicate approximate location), interactions with ads and the app (such as app launches and taps), diagnostic information about app and SDK performance, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. This data is encrypted in transit.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Ad privacy settings in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. MesejPantas Pro has no ads.

4. Purchases

MesejPantas Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro has been purchased on your Google account so it can unlock it.

5. Children

MesejPantas is meant for adults, such as parents sending notes to teachers. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

7. Contact
