Cloud Catcher – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Cloud Catcher (“the app”) is a simple game for children, made by husnitalip (“we”). We care about children’s privacy. This policy explains what happens to data when the app is used.

What we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to enter a name, email, photo or any other personal details. The game’s best score is stored only on the device and never leaves it.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob to keep it free. All ad requests are marked as coming from a child, so that:

  • no personalised (interest-based) ads are shown, and no ad remarketing is used;
  • the Android advertising ID is not collected (the permission is removed from the app);
  • only ads rated suitable for general audiences (“G”) are shown, from ad sources certified for Google Play’s Families program.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process limited technical information such as IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions and crash/diagnostic data. This is handled by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ads are shown only on the menu, pause and results screens, never while a child is playing. Ads can be closed and do not ask children to buy anything.

Children

The app is designed for children and follows Google Play’s Families Policy and the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe any personal information was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Permissions

The app only uses internet access (to load ads). It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location services or storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
