KTM Train Utara

Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Summary

KTM Train Utara ("the app") does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Location

When you tap Start tracking, the app uses your device's precise location to find your nearest station, detect which line you are on and your direction of travel, and send next-station alerts.

While tracking is on, location continues to be used when the app is in the background or the screen is off, so alerts keep working. A persistent notification is shown the whole time, and tracking stops when you tap Stop tracking.

Location is processed only on your device. It is never saved, uploaded or shared with anyone, including the developer.

Notifications

Next-station, approaching-station and alight-alarm notifications are generated locally on your device. No push-notification service or server is used.

Settings stored on your device

Your preferences (alert options, home station, theme) are saved locally on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Map tiles

The Map tab downloads map images from OpenStreetMap tile servers. As with any web request, those servers receive your IP address and the map area being viewed; your GPS position is not sent. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact
