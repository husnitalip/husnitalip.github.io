Kabus – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Kabus ("the app") helps visitors avoid traffic in Cameron Highlands, Malaysia, with a congestion forecast, live road times, road reports and parking status. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Summary

There is no account and no sign-in. The app does not read your location, contacts, photos or files. Road and parking reports you choose to send are anonymous. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects device information as described below.

Reports you send

  • Road reports: the type of problem (for example jam, accident, fog), the spot you place on the map, and an optional short note. Other users can see the report for 2 hours.
  • Parking status: which car park and whether it is full, has a few spaces or has space.
  • Anonymous device ID: a random ID created by the app and stored on your phone. It is sent with each report only so the server can block spam (for example, many reports from one phone in a few minutes). It is not linked to your name, phone number or Google account, and it is never shown to other users.

Reports are stored on our server (hosted by Supabase) and deleted automatically after 7 days.

Location

The app does not ask for or read your phone's location. A road report uses the point you place on the map yourself.

Maps and traffic data

Map images are loaded from OpenFreeMap, using OpenStreetMap data. Like any website, the map server receives your device's IP address when tiles are downloaded. Live travel times are fetched by our server from TomTom; no information about you is sent to TomTom.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Ads may be personalised unless you opt out. In regions where the law requires it (such as the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy choices" in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Data shared

We do not sell your data. Information is processed only by the services above (Supabase for hosting reports, Google AdMob for ads, OpenFreeMap for map tiles) to run the app.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Reports are deleted automatically after 7 days. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the anonymous device ID from your phone. To ask for a report to be removed sooner, email us with the approximate time and place of the report.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
