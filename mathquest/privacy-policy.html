Math Quest: Kids Fun Games – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Math Quest: Kids Fun Games (“the app”, package com.mathematicskid.mathquest) is an educational maths game for children and families, made by husnitalip (“we”). We care about children’s privacy. This policy explains what happens to data when the app is used.

What we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to enter a name, email, photo or any other personal details. Game stars, badges, sound settings and the age group chosen on the start screen are stored only on the device and never leave it.

Age screen

When the app first opens, a neutral age screen asks which age group the player is in. No ads are requested and the ads SDK is not started until this screen is completed. The answer is kept only on the device and is used only to choose the right ad settings below.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob to keep it free. Ad settings depend on the age group chosen:

  • Under 13: ad requests are marked as coming from a child, personalised (interest-based) ads are turned off and only ads rated suitable for general audiences (“G”) are shown.
  • 13–17: ad requests are marked as coming from a teen, personalised ads are turned off and ads are limited to a “PG” rating.
  • 18 and over: ads are limited to a “PG” rating. Where required by law, Google’s consent form is shown so you can choose whether ads may be personalised.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process limited technical information such as IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions and crash/diagnostic data. This is handled by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ads appear at natural breaks, such as the end of a level, and optional reward ads are shown only when a player chooses to watch one for bonus hints. Ads can be closed and do not ask children to buy anything.

Children

The app is designed for children and follows Google Play’s Families Policy and the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe any personal information was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Parent controls

Links that leave the app and the Parent Zone are protected by a parental gate. In the Parent Zone, a parent can change the age group or erase all game progress and settings saved on the device. Uninstalling the app also removes this data.

Permissions

The app only uses internet and network-state access (to load ads). It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location services or storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
