SahamKu – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · App: SahamKu (com.husnitalip.sahamku) · Developer: Husni Talip

Summary

SahamKu does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. Your portfolio stays on your phone.

Information stored on your device

The stocks, purchase dates, quantities, prices, fees, exchange rates and notes you enter are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. So are your display preferences (for example, MYR or USD). The developer cannot access this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Information sent over the internet

To show live prices, the app requests quotes and exchange rates from public market-data services: Yahoo Finance (finance.yahoo.com) and, as a fallback, Google Finance (google.com/finance). These requests contain only the stock symbol or search term (for example "AAPL" or "Maybank") and the currency pair. They never contain your quantities, prices or other portfolio details. Like any internet request, they also include your device's IP address and basic technical headers. Those services handle that information under their own privacy policies:

  • Yahoo Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

All requests are made over encrypted HTTPS connections.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, photos or files.
  • No personal data is sold or shared with third parties.

Children

SahamKu is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Not financial advice

Prices may be delayed and may be inaccurate. SahamKu is a tracking tool only and does not provide investment advice or trading services.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Dasar Privasi (Bahasa Melayu)

SahamKu tidak mengumpul, menyimpan di pelayan, menjual atau berkongsi data peribadi anda. Semua maklumat portfolio yang anda masukkan (saham, tarikh beli, kuantiti, harga, yuran, kadar tukaran dan nota) disimpan hanya dalam storan peribadi aplikasi pada peranti anda. Maklumat ini dipadam apabila aplikasi dinyahpasang.

Untuk memaparkan harga terkini, aplikasi menghantar permintaan ke Yahoo Finance dan Google Finance (sandaran). Permintaan ini mengandungi simbol saham atau carian sahaja, tanpa butiran portfolio anda. Semua sambungan menggunakan HTTPS. Tiada iklan, analitik, akaun atau penjejakan. Aplikasi ini untuk pengguna dewasa (18+) dan bukan nasihat pelaburan.
