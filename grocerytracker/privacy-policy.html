Grocery Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Grocery Tracker ("the app") is an offline expense tracker for grocery receipts. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

The app has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. Everything you enter or scan stays on your device. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Information stored on your device

  • Purchases you enter or scan: store name, date, location text, items, prices and totals. Stored in a local database on your phone.
  • Receipt photos: photos you take or pick are copied into the app's private storage so you can view them later.

Receipt scanning

Text on receipt photos is read on your device using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. Photos are not uploaded to us or to any server by the app.

Location (optional)

If you tap "Detect current location", the app asks for location permission and uses your approximate or precise location once, to suggest a mall or neighbourhood name for the purchase. To turn coordinates into a place name, Android's system geocoding service (provided by Google Play services) may process the coordinates. The app does not send your location to us and does not track you in the background. You can deny the permission and type the location yourself.

Camera and photos

The camera is opened through your phone's camera app. Gallery images are chosen with the Android photo picker, so the app only receives the images you select.

Exporting data

When you use "Export monthly CSV", the app creates a file and opens the Android share sheet. You decide where it goes. We do not receive it.

Backups

If you have Android backup enabled, your phone's database and receipt photos may be included in your Google account backup, under Google's own privacy terms.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Delete a receipt inside the app to remove it and its items. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all data held by the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
